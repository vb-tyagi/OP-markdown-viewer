# The case for slow mornings

The first hour of the day decides who is steering.

If it starts with a feed, the feed is steering. If it starts with email, the sender is steering. Neither of them has any idea what you were trying to do this year.

## Three rules I actually keep

1. **No screens before coffee.** The coffee is a timer, not a ritual. It takes eight minutes and that is enough of a gap.
2. **One sentence on paper.** What would make today feel finished? Not a list. One sentence.
3. **The first task is chosen the night before.** Choosing in the morning is where the drift begins.

## What it is not

It is not a productivity system, and it is not a wellness routine. I don't meditate, and I have no opinion about cold showers.

It is closer to a *boundary*. The world gets me at nine, not at seven.

## The honest cost

Some mornings the sentence on paper is wrong, and I spend the first hour on the wrong thing. That still beats spending it on someone else's thing.
